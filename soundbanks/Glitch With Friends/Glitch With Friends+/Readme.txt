Hello, and thank you for downloading the Glitch With Friends sample pack!

I wanted to put put together a sample pack aimed primarily at glitch and IDM producers. Originally this was a solo project but I realized I have a number of friends who are very talented producers and sound designers in their own right, and the sample pack is that much better off for including all of their contributions. It turned into a very fun collaborative project, one that I hope allows you to glitch with your friends as much as I was able to glitch with mine.

This pack contains a variety of different samples, ranging from standard electronic drums, to bizarre percussive one-shots, broken glitched noises, warping neuro bass cuts, wavetables, a large variety of loops, and even bizarre textural noise.

A wide variety of sound-design techniques are represented in these samples. These range from iterative granular synthesis, salvaging data from broken hard drives, time-stretch manipulation, wacky synthesis, and many other cutting edge techniques. All wavetables were prepared by Glorkglunk, though the ones labeled with other artists' names were made from specific samples from this pack itself. This type of recursive iteration is a common paradigm among those of us who created this pack.

Thank you again for downloading!

-Man From Sol
